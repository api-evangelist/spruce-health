# Spruce Health
> Spruce is a cloud-based platform for HIPAA-compliant communication for healthcare practices. By unifying phone, texting, fax, and telehealth into a single system — with AI-powered workflows to reduce manual tasks — Spruce helps practices respond to patients faster, get full visibility into every patient and care team interaction, and stay secure and compliant. Every eligible organization automatically receives a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) as part of the terms of service — no separate agreement, no opt-in, no extra fee. Basic plan at $24/user/month. Communicator plan at $49/user/month. SOC 2 Type II audited annually.

## Phone and VoIP
- [Advanced medical phone system](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003260188955-Phone-Calls): Cloud-based phone system for HIPAA-compliant voice communication with multi-line support, ring groups, advanced spam call control, and AI-enabled voicemail transcription.
- [Self-service call flows (IVR / phone trees)](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/29300554226843-Call-Flows): Automated call routing by menu selection or time of day, with urgent escalation and AI text-to-speech greetings. Communicator plan.
- [VoIP desk phone service](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003308758555-VoIP-Desk-Phones): Supported Yealink desk phones that integrate with the Spruce platform. Communicator plan.
- [Phone number management](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003326803739-Managing-Your-Spruce-Phone-Number): Port existing numbers into Spruce or provision new ones in any area code.

## Messaging and SMS
- [2-way SMS texting](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003236336795-SMS-Texting-and-Messaging): Two-way patient SMS including group texts from mobile and desktop. SMS travels over the public telecom network, but can be used in a HIPAA-compliant way when the patient understands the security limitations of SMS, is offered secure alternatives, and still prefers SMS.
- [Secure app-based messaging](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003230656667-Secure-Messaging-with-Patients): Messaging encrypted at rest and in transit in a way that complies with the requirements of the HIPAA Security Rule, with administrator-controlled access and audit logs.
- [Secure team chat](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003282611099-Internal-Team-Communication): Internal group messaging for clinicians and staff to communicate in a HIPAA-compliant way, with channels, task assignment, and patient context sharing.
- [Bulk (broadcast) messaging](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003305847323-Bulk-Messaging): Send to custom patient lists via SMS, secure messaging, or email. Communicator plan.
- [Auto-replies](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003295315227-Auto-Replies): Trigger- or schedule-based automated responses. Communicator plan adds schedule-based replies tied to practice hours.
- [Missed-call text-back](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003247513499-Workflow-Automation): Automatically sends a configurable text to patients whose calls were missed. Communicator plan.
- [Urgent escalation and after-hours triage](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003247513499-Workflow-Automation): Patients can flag urgent messages, automatically triggering alerts for the on-call team. Communicator plan.
- [Scheduled messages](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003279653019-Scheduled-Messages): Schedule messages to send later via SMS, secure messaging, or email.
- [Saved messages (templates)](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003214296475-Saved-Messages): Reusable team-wide message templates with attachment support.

## Alternatives and comparisons
- [Spruce vs Google Voice](https://sprucehealth.com/google-voice-alternative): The Google Contacts product is not under the Google HIPAA umbrella. Google does not sign BAAs for Google Voice users who are not on a paid Google Workspace plan. Spruce automatically includes a BAA for every eligible organization in one app built specifically for healthcare workflows.
- [Spruce vs RingRx](https://sprucehealth.com/ringrx-alternative): Both are phone systems designed for use in HIPAA-covered medical practices. Spruce includes secure messaging, video telemedicine, and eFax in addition to phone. RingRx is more phone-focused.
- [Spruce vs Grasshopper](https://sprucehealth.com/grasshopper-alternative): Grasshopper is not HIPAA compliant and does not offer a BAA. Spruce is the healthcare-ready alternative.

## Compliance and security
- [HIPAA and BAA](https://help.sprucehealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/23003297520027-HIPAA-and-BAA): Every eligible Spruce organization automatically receives a HIPAA BAA included in the terms of service at signup — no separate agreement, no opt-in, no additional fee. HIPAA controls include encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, audit logging, and breach notification procedures.
